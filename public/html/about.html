About Bonsai Mori

Bonsai Mori is a bonsai business with many years of experience in growing, shaping, and caring for bonsai trees. We are passionate about creating beautiful bonsai that bring the beauty and tranquility of nature into everyday spaces. Our main collection focuses on four popular types of bonsai: Juniper Bonsai, Pine Bonsai, Ficus Bonsai, and Carmona Bonsai. Each type has its own unique shape, style, and character, making every tree special in its own way. We also offer other varieties from time to time, depending on availability and seasonal opportunities. Every tree is carefully selected and maintained with attention to detail, quality, and health.

Our Bonsai Mori Services

Bonsai Mori provides a range of services, including custom bonsai design, pruning, and tree shaping. Customers can discuss their preferred size, shape, and style with our team to create a bonsai that suits their personal taste. We also offer advice on bonsai care, helping customers understand how to maintain their trees and keep them healthy. Our experienced team is ready to provide guidance and support throughout the design and care process. Customers who are interested in visiting, discussing a custom design, or learning more about our services can make an appointment in advance. We aim to provide a friendly and personalized experience for everyone who appreciates the art of bonsai.
